# Incident Response Runbook

Assign severity, incident commander, technical lead, communications owner, and scribe. Open a restricted incident channel; do not place secrets or personal data in public issues.

1. Detect and validate the symptom.
2. Contain impact while preserving evidence.
3. Establish a UTC timeline of alerts, deploys, decisions, and system changes.
4. Mitigate with rollback or forward recovery according to schema compatibility.
5. Verify recovery externally and monitor for recurrence.
6. Notify stakeholders at a cadence appropriate to severity.
7. Close only after follow-up owners and due dates are recorded.

Severity 1 means widespread unavailability, confirmed data exposure, or integrity loss. Severity 2 means major degraded service with a workaround. Severity 3 means limited impact without security or data-integrity risk.
